Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

ABC Rides connects people travelling between cities in Pakistan so they can share a car and its costs. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it and how to delete it.

Who can see it

  • Other users see your name, traveller type, gender, organisation, bio, rating and whether your ID is verified. Your phone number is shown only to the driver or passengers of a booking that is confirmed.
  • ABC Rides staff see ID documents only to review them. Uploaded documents are never public.
  • Service providers: our hosting provider stores the data; an SMS provider receives your phone number to send verification codes; a mapping service receives place coordinates (not your identity) to work out road distances.
  • Authorities, only when Pakistani law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell personal data and we do not show ads.

Security

Data travels over encrypted HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Repeated wrong logins are blocked for a while. ID documents are only reachable by administrators.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, personal details and documents are removed straight away; trip, payment and report records are kept without your name for up to 3 years for accounting, safety and legal reasons.

Deleting your account

  1. Cancel any upcoming rides or bookings.
  2. In the app go to Profile → Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.

This removes your name, phone, email, CNIC, ID photos, vehicle and notifications and signs you out on every device. If you can no longer log in, report it from the app using another account or contact us at the email on our Google Play page, from the phone or email on your account, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

ABC Rides is for people aged 18 and over. Under-18s may only travel with a parent or guardian who holds the account.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
