🚗 ABC Rides

Intercity rides for commuters, students & travellers

Testing on Wi-Fi: run npm start on your computer, then enter its address, e.g. http://192.168.1.10:3000. The phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi.

Hosted server: enter its https address, e.g. https://abc-rides.onrender.com.

You can change this later from Profile → Change server.
