Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above. Chrome may warn that the file can harm your device: tap Download anyway. (It says this for every app that is not from the Play Store yet.)
  2. Open the downloaded abc-rides.apk. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for Chrome (or your file manager), then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect shows a pop-up, tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open ABC Rides. The first start can take up to a minute while the server wakes up.

iPhone or computer? Use Open in your browser, then Share → Add to Home Screen for an app icon.

Create your account

  1. Open the app and tap Profile → Sign up.
  2. Verify your phone number with the code we send.
  3. Passengers can book straight away. Drivers register once with their CNIC, licence and car; we review it, usually the same day.

What to try

  • Passenger: search Lahore → Islamabad, open a ride, pick your stop, book a seat, chat with the driver.
  • Driver: tap Offer, pick the route and stops, see the suggested fair fare and your earnings, publish.
  • Partial trips: search Lahore → Gujranwala to find a seat on a Lahore → Sialkot ride.
  • Safety: the SOS button, emergency contact and trip sharing on a booked ride.
