1. What ABC Rides is

ABC Rides is a platform where private drivers who are already making an intercity trip offer their empty seats, and passengers share the running costs. ABC Rides is not a transport company, does not employ drivers and is not a party to the agreement between a driver and passengers.

2. Your account

  • Keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • We may suspend accounts that break these terms, are reported for unsafe behaviour or have a very low reliability score.

3. Drivers

  • You need a valid driving licence, a roadworthy, insured vehicle in your name or used with the owner's permission, and our approval before offering rides.
  • Fares must stay within the per-km limits shown in the app. They are meant to share costs, not to run a commercial taxi service.
  • Follow traffic law, don't drive tired, and don't carry more passengers than seat belts.

4. Passengers

  • Be at the pickup point on time, pay the agreed fare and treat the driver and car with respect.
  • Women-only rides are for women passengers only.

5. Fees, wallet and refunds

  • Posting a ride is free. When a booking is confirmed, ABC Rides may charge the driver a commission and the passenger a booking fee, as shown in the app before you confirm. The first few confirmations can be free.
  • Fees are taken from your ABC Rides wallet. Top-ups are added after we verify the payment.
  • The fare itself is paid directly to the driver (cash, JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank) unless the app says otherwise.
  • If the other side cancels a confirmed trip, your fee is refunded to your wallet. Cancelling yourself, especially close to departure, lowers your reliability score and may lead to extra fees.
  • Wallet balance can be withdrawn on request when you close your account, less any amount you owe.

6. Safety

We verify phones and IDs and approve drivers, but we cannot guarantee anyone's behaviour. Check the profile, rating and verification badges before you travel, share your trip with family, and use SOS or call 15 in an emergency. Report anything unsafe in the app.
